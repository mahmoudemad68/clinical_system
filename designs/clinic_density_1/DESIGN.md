## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for high-stakes medical environments where information density and clarity are paramount. The brand personality is clinical, authoritative, and precise. It draws from **Modern Corporate** and **Systematic** design movements, prioritizing utility over decoration.

The aesthetic, "Clinic Density," focuses on maximizing vertical and horizontal space to minimize scrolling, allowing doctors to synthesize patient data and AI insights rapidly. The emotional response is one of controlled confidence and institutional trust. 

**Key Principles:**
- **Information Rigor:** No wasted padding; every pixel serves a diagnostic or communicative purpose.
- **Trust Architecture:** Explicit visual signaling for AI-generated data versus human-verified data.
- **Urgency Awareness:** Distinctive styling for critical deadlines and clinical alerts without inducing "alarm fatigue."

## Colors

The palette is anchored by **Clinic Teal (#008080)**, a color synonymous with medical professionalism and sterilized environments. It is used for primary actions and human-verified states.

**Functional Color Application:**
- **Primary (Clinic Teal):** Navigational anchors, primary buttons, and verified clinical notes.
- **Secondary (Midnight):** High-contrast text and structural borders to ground the UI.
- **Tertiary (Indigo):** Reserved exclusively for **AI provenance**. Anything generated by the LLM uses indigo accents to differentiate from the teal human entries.
- **Neutral:** A range of cool grays (Slate) to define surface hierarchies without adding visual warmth that might distract from data.
- **Semantic Accents:** Urgent deadlines use a high-chroma red. Encryption and read-only states use a "Secure Green" icon set.

## Typography

The typographic system utilizes a tri-font strategy to separate intent:
1. **Hanken Grotesk (Headings):** Sharp, contemporary, and professional. Used for patient names and section headers.
2. **Inter (UI & Body):** Highly legible at small sizes. Used for the bulk of clinical notes and communication logs.
3. **JetBrains Mono (Technical Labels):** Used for metadata, timestamps, encryption status, and provenance tags (e.g., [AI GENERATED]).

**Clinical Readability Rules:**
- Use `body-sm` (13px) for dense data tables to increase row visibility.
- AI-generated text should use a slightly increased line-height (22px) and a subtle italicization or Indigo color tint to ensure it is never confused with physician entries.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system employs a **Fixed Grid** model optimized for 1440px dashboards, with a high-density 4px base unit. 

**Layout Rules:**
- **Density:** Standard padding is reduced to 8px for list items and cards to maximize the "at-a-glance" data threshold.
- **Sidebar:** A collapsed 64px icon rail or expanded 240px navigation.
- **AI Panel:** A persistent right-hand "Intelligence" tray (320px) that slides over content rather than reflowing it, maintaining the doctor's focus on the primary chart.
- **Breakpoints:** 
  - Desktop: 1200px+ (3-column: Nav, Content, AI)
  - Tablet: 768px - 1199px (2-column: Nav, Content)
  - Mobile: 375px+ (Single column, AI relegated to a bottom sheet).

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain a professional, "flat" clinical feel, this design system avoids heavy shadows. It uses **Tonal Layers** and **Low-Contrast Outlines**.

- **Level 0 (Background):** #F8FAFC (Slate 50).
- **Level 1 (Cards/Surface):** White with a 1px border (#E2E8F0). No shadow.
- **Level 2 (Modals/Popovers):** White with a 1px border (#CBD5E1) and a very tight 4px blur shadow (Opacity 0.05) to provide just enough lift to separate from the background.
- **AI Streaming State:** Elements currently being "written" by AI feature a subtle indigo outer glow (2px) to indicate active generation.

## Shapes

The shape language is **Soft (0.25rem)**. 

- **Clinical Precision:** Larger radii feel too "consumer-oriented" or "playful." Small 4px corners maintain a disciplined, structural look.
- **Interactive Elements:** Buttons and Input fields use the 4px standard. 
- **AI Components:** AI "Scope Notices" or "Provenance Chips" use a slightly larger 8px radius (`rounded-lg`) to distinctively mark them as separate from the standard medical record geometry.

## Components

**Buttons & Actions:**
- **Primary:** Clinic Teal background, white text. Small (28px height) or Medium (36px height).
- **AI Action:** Indigo background with a subtle "sparkle" icon.
- **Read-Only:** Grayed-out state with a "Lock" icon. Clicking triggers a tooltip explaining the provenance/permissions.

**AI Communication Features:**
- **Streaming Indicator:** A 2px indigo line that pulses at the top of the container while the AI is generating.
- **Provenance Tag:** Every AI block must be capped with a `label-caps` JetBrains Mono tag: `[AI SOURCE: SYSTEM v2.4]`.
- **Scope Notice:** A pale indigo banner at the start of AI interactions clearly stating the AI's limitations.

**Communication Logs:**
- **Encryption Indicator:** A small green shield icon next to the timestamp in all message threads.
- **Deadline Chips:** High-contrast red pills for tasks due in <2 hours; amber for <24 hours.

**Input Fields:**
- Minimalist 1px borders. Focus state uses a 2px Clinic Teal ring. Error states use a 1px red border with no glow to prevent "bleeding" into adjacent dense data.
